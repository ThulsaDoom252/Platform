🧠 Grammar: have & have got

Ідея: have і have got означають одне й те саме — «у мене є…». Українською ми кажемо «У МЕНЕ є книжка», а англійською: I have a book — я маю книжку. Пам'ятаєш SVO? S = I, V = have, O = a book.
havehave got
+I / you / we / they have a car.
he / she / it has a car.		I / you / we / they have got ('ve got) a car.
he / she / it has got ('s got) a car.
−I don't have a car.
She doesn't have a car.		I haven't got a car.
She hasn't got a car.
?Do you have a car?
Does she have a car?		Have you got a car?
Has she got a car?
Short answersYes, I do. / No, I don't.
Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.		Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
Yes, she has. / No, she hasn't.
Як вибрати? have — звичайне дієслово, працює з do / does (як like, live, need, want). have got — сам будує питання й заперечення, БЕЗ do / does. Обидва варіанти правильні, have got звучить більш розмовно і британськи.

👤 have got + зовнішність

She has got long hair. — У неї довге волосся.
He's got a beard. — У нього борода.
Ben doesn't have a moustache. — У Бена немає вусів.

⚠️ 's got = has got, а не is! She's got a car = She has got a car. Але She's tall = She is tall.
⚠️ Вік — не have! Мені 30 = I'm thirty.
⚠️ have breakfast (снідати) — тут тільки have, без got.

⚠️ Типові помилки

❌ She have a car. ✅ She has a car.
❌ Do you have got a TV? ✅ Do you have a TV? / Have you got a TV?
❌ She doesn't has a wallet. ✅ She doesn't have a wallet.
❌ I don't have got a car. ✅ I haven't got a car.
❌ Has she a computer? ✅ Has she got a computer? / Does she have a computer?
❌ I have twenty-eight. ✅ I'm twenty-eight.

📖 Text: Emma and Her Things

1Hi! My name is Emma. I am twenty-eight. I am from London, but I live in Kyiv. I have a job at a bank. I like my job, and I am very hard-working.

2I have got a small house. It is quiet, and I like it. I haven't got a TV. I don't need a TV — I have a computer. My computer is old, but it's OK. I need it for email and the internet.

3I have got a car, but it is very old, and I don't like it. Actually, I like the bus!

4My backpack is very big. My wallet, my keys, my mobile phone and a book are in it. Do I need a book at work? No, I don't. But I like books! My umbrella is in my backpack too.

5My friend Ben is a doctor. He is in his thirties. He is tall and handsome, and he has got a beard. Ben is funny and very kind, but he is a bit lazy! Ben has got a new car. His car is big.

6Today it's raining, and Ben is in my house. He hasn't got an umbrella. «Emma, is this your umbrella?» «Yes, it's mine. Do you need it?» «Yes, I do!» So Ben has got my umbrella now.

💬 Dialogue 1: Is This Your Wallet?

Max працює в супермаркеті. Olivia — покупниця.

1Max: Good evening! Is this your wallet?

2Olivia: Sorry?

3Max: This wallet. Is it yours?

4Olivia: No, I don't think so. My wallet is in my backpack.

5Max: OK…

6Olivia: Well… Oh no, my wallet isn't in my backpack! Yes, it's mine! Thank you!

7Max: Have you got your keys and your mobile phone?

8Olivia: Yes, I have. My keys are here, and my phone is here too.

9Max: Good. And your umbrella? It's raining.

10Olivia: No, I haven't got an umbrella today.

11Max: We have umbrellas here at the supermarket. Do you need an umbrella?

12Olivia: Yes, I do! I need a big umbrella. Thank you!

13Max: You're welcome. Have a nice day!

14Olivia: You too! Take care.

💬 Dialogue 2: The Man with the Umbrella

Emma дзвонить своєму другу Ben.

1Ben: Hi, Emma! What's up?

2Emma: Hey, Ben! Not much. Well… actually, I need your help.

3Ben: OK, I'm here.

4Emma: My umbrella isn't in my house. Have you got it?

5Ben: Your umbrella? No, I haven't. I don't think so.

6Emma: Anyway, a man at the bank has got an umbrella, and I think it's mine!

7Ben: Come again? A man at the bank has got your umbrella?

8Emma: Yes! I think so.

9Ben: What's his name?

10Emma: I don't know.

11Ben: OK. What does he look like? Is he tall?

12Emma: Yes, he is. He's very tall and handsome.

13Ben: How old is he?

14Emma: I think he's in his thirties.

15Ben: Has he got a beard?

16Emma: No, he hasn't. But he's got a moustache.

17Ben: And his hair? Is it long?

18Emma: No, it isn't. His hair is short.

19Ben: What is he like? Is he rude?

20Emma: No, he isn't. He's friendly and funny.

21Ben: Tall, handsome, a moustache, short hair… Has he got a job at the supermarket?

22Emma: I don't know. I think so. Why?

23Ben: I think his name is Max. He's my friend! He's very kind and honest.

24Emma: Honest? But he's got my umbrella!

25Ben: Well… Actually, Emma… your umbrella is in my car. Sorry!

26Emma: In your car?! Oh, Ben! So the umbrella at the bank is his umbrella!

27Ben: I think so. Sorry, I've got to go. See you later!

28Emma: Bye, Ben. And I need my umbrella!
