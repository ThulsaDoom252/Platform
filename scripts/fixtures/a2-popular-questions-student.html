📖 Vocabulary · 9 Popular Questions

🧩 Слова-помічники (нові): a language → languages — мова · English, Polish, German, Spanish, Italian — англійська, польська, німецька, іспанська, італійська · a little — трохи · a hobby → hobbies — хобі · free time — вільний час · a phone number — номер телефону · double — подвійний · oh / zero — нуль · would like = 'd like — хотів(-ла) б · reading, dancing, drawing, travelling, swimming — читання, танці, малювання, подорожі, плавання · a chef — шеф-кухар · a neighbour — сусід · a building — будинок (багатоповерховий) · polite — ввічливий · a cake — торт
🌍 Where are you from? — Звідки ти?
📌 from — у кінці питання! ✗ From where are you?
  • I'm from Ukraine.
  • I'm from Kyiv, but now I live in Lviv.
🎂 How old are you? — Скільки тобі років?
📌 ✗ I have 30 years → ✓ I'm 30. ⚠️ Дорослим незнайомим людям це питання краще не ставити — it's not polite.
  • I'm twenty-nine.
  • I'm thirty years old.
🗣️ What languages do you speak? — Якими мовами ти розмовляєш?
📌 speak + мова, без in: ✗ speak in English → ✓ speak English. a little — трохи.
  • I speak Ukrainian and English.
  • I speak Ukrainian, English and a little Polish.
💼 Do you have a job? — У тебе є робота?
📌 Відповідь: Yes, I do / No, I don't. Згадай: He is a… = He works as a…
  • Yes, I do. I work as a housekeeper.
  • No, I don't. I'm between jobs right now. / I'm a student.
⭐ What job would you like to have? — Ким ти хотів(-ла) би працювати?
📌 would like = 'd like = хотів би · would like + TO + V: ✗ I'd like be → ✓ I'd like to be
  • I'd like to be a chef.
  • I'd like to work as a teacher.
📞 What is your phone number? — Який твій номер телефону?
📌 Цифри — по одній. 0 = oh (або zero) · 55 = double five
  • It's oh-six-seven, one-two-three, four-five, double eight.
  • My phone number is 093 555 21 40.
🏠 Where do you live? — Де ти живеш?
📌 ✗ I am live → ✓ I live. in + місто / країна / квартира
  • I live in Kyiv.
  • I live in a small apartment in the city.
🍽️ What is your favourite food? — Яка твоя улюблена їжа?
📌 favourite (UK) = favorite (US)
  • My favourite food is meatballs.
  • I love fried fish with potatoes.
🎨 What are your hobbies? — Які в тебе хобі?
📌 a hobby → hobbies → What ARE…? · like + V-ing: I like cooking.
  • My hobbies are cooking and dancing.
  • In my free time I like reading and playing board games.
💡 Після відповіді — поверни питання: And you? / What about you? — так розмова не зупиняється.
💡 Порядок у питанні: Question word + be + subject → Where are you from? · Question word + do + subject + V → Where do you live?

🧩 Revision · can / should / had better

МодальнеЗначенняПриклад
can / can'tвмію, можу, можна (прохання)I can speak English. Can you give me your number?
should / shouldn'tварто — загальна порада, думкаYou should learn English every day.
had better / 'd better notкраще б — конкретна ситуація зараз + наслідокYou'd better go now, or you'll be late.
⚠️ Після всіх трьох — V без to: can speak · should learn · 'd better go.
⚠️ ✗ would better → ✓ had better.

📄 Text · The New Neighbour

1 Last week a new neighbour came to live in Lucy and Mark's building. On Saturday Lucy was going to the market when a young woman said, “Hi! I'm Olivia. I live in apartment 12.” “Nice to meet you! I'm Lucy. Where are you from?” asked Lucy.

2 “I'm from Poland, but now I live here, in Kyiv,” said Olivia. She is twenty-eight years old. She speaks Polish, English and a little Ukrainian. “I'd like to speak Ukrainian better,” she said. “If I had more time, I would learn it every day!”

3 Lucy asked, “Do you have a job?” “Yes, I do. I work as a housekeeper in a hotel. But I'd like to be a chef. I love cooking!” Olivia's favourite food is meatballs with potatoes. “You should try Mark's meatballs,” said Lucy. “They're better than meatballs in restaurants!”

4 “What are your hobbies?” asked Olivia. “I like cooking and playing board games,” said Lucy. “Me too!” said Olivia. “In my free time I also like travelling and dancing.”

5 Then Lucy said, “Oh no, I'd better go now, or there won't be any fresh tomatoes at the market! What is your phone number?” “It's oh-nine-seven, four-two-one, double six, eight-three,” said Olivia. Lucy put it in her smartphone.

6 In the evening Olivia came to Lucy and Mark's place. They were playing board games and eating meatballs all evening. “If you come to my place next Saturday, I'll cook Polish food for you,” said Olivia. “It was nice to meet you!” “Likewise!” said Lucy and Mark.

💬 Dialogue 1 · Nice to Meet You

1Jack: Emily, this is my cousin, Ben.

2Emily: Hi, Ben! Nice to meet you.

3Ben: Nice to meet you too!

4Emily: Where are you from, Ben?

5Ben: I'm from the UK, from London. But my mom is Ukrainian.

6Emily: Oh! What languages do you speak?

7Ben: English and a little Ukrainian. I can understand a lot, but I can't speak well.

8Emily: You should speak Ukrainian with Jack every day!

9Ben: Ha! Jack always speaks English with me.

10Emily: So, do you have a job?

11Ben: Yes, I do. I'm a programmer. But I'd like to be a chef one day.

12Emily: A chef? What is your favourite food?

13Ben: Ukrainian food, for sure! Borscht and meatballs.

14Jack: If you come to my place on Sunday, my mom will make borscht for you.

15Ben: Great! Then I'd better not have breakfast on Sunday!

💬 Dialogue 2 · At the Birthday Party

1Tom: Kate, this is my friend Daniel. Daniel, this is Kate.

2Daniel: Hi, Kate! Nice to meet you.

3Kate: Nice to meet you too! So, how do you know Tom?

4Daniel: We were at school together.

5Kate: Oh, great! Where do you live?

6Daniel: I live in a small village near Kyiv. I have a house with a big garden.

7Kate: Wow! Do you grow vegetables?

8Daniel: Yes, I do — potatoes, tomatoes and cucumbers. And a lot of strawberries!

9Kate: Tom's grandma grows strawberries too. Do you have a job?

10Daniel: Yes, I'm a builder. I work in the city on weekdays.

11Kate: Do you like your job?

12Daniel: It's OK, but I'd like to be a teacher one day. I love children.

13Kate: A teacher? What would you teach?

14Daniel: Languages! I speak English, German and a little Spanish.

15Kate: Three languages! You should be a teacher, for sure!

16Daniel: Thanks! And what are your hobbies, Kate?

17Kate: I like cooking, dancing and playing board games.

18Daniel: Board games? Me too! If you are free on Friday, you can play with us. We play every Friday at my place.

19Kate: Sure! What is your phone number?

20Daniel: It's oh-six-seven, three-five-two, double one, nine-four.

21Kate: Great, thanks. And how old are you? Oh, sorry, I shouldn't ask that!

22Daniel: Ha! No problem. I'm thirty-one.

23Tom: Kate, Daniel! The cake is on the table. You'd better come now, or there won't be any cake for you!

24Kate: We're coming!
